Privacy policy

Last updated November 12, 2026.

What the games save

Games on Turbo Play save your progress, such as coins, cars and best times, in your own browser. That information stays on your device. We don't collect your name, email or other personal details, and there are no accounts.

Ads

Turbo Play is free because it shows ads. Our advertising partners, such as Google, may use cookies to show ads and measure how well they work. You can learn how Google uses this information at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Children

Our games are made for players of all ages. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children, and we ask our ad partners to treat this site as directed at children so they show non-personalized ads.
